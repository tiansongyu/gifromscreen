## Remaining release risks

- Export-generated transition frames are not yet included in editor playback.
- GNOME and KDE Portal/PipeWire sessions, mixed-DPI desktops, and long-running native capture still need compositor-specific acceptance.
- During automation, forcibly destroying the X11 window with `xdotool windowclose` (XDestroyWindow, not a normal close request) triggered a `winit` 0.30.13 `BadWindow` panic in its coordinate lookup. Normal close requests exit cleanly. This external-window-destruction robustness issue remains recorded for release investigation; no blanket "all bugs fixed" claim is made.
- Remaining source/editor/release features are listed in [LINUX-STATUS.md](LINUX-STATUS.md) and the baseline [PARITY-AUDIT.md](PARITY-AUDIT.md), including webcam/board/video sources, expanded effects and automation, packaging, localization, and accessibility acceptance.

## Implementation simplifications

Text shaping is isolated in a bounded worker-owned Rust crate; export reads immutable pixels. Caption/title editing lives outside the desktop entry point. Frame, overlay, and mask consumers share one validated raster descriptor rather than duplicating conflicting role checks. The inspector and playback/timeline controls have separate layout entry points, and text/image/shape/drawing/layer tools are exposed as focused choices. These are concrete maintainability and interaction improvements; they do not imply personal endorsement by Linus Torvalds or Apple.
